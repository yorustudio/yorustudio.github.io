Privacy Policy

YORU: Just Drift · Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YORU Studio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Android game YORU: Just Drift (the "Game"). It also explains how third-party advertising technology may process information when the Game displays ads.

1. Information and local game data

The Game may store gameplay settings, progress, and unlock information locally on your device to support its features. We do not currently provide a YORU Studio account system or a user-profile upload service in the Game. Local gameplay data is distinct from information that advertising providers may process.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The Game integrates Google AdMob, provided by Google, for advertising. Depending on your device, region, privacy choices, and ad availability, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers;
  • IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, and device characteristics;
  • Ad interactions, impressions, diagnostics, and information used to measure performance, prevent fraud, or deliver ads.

The specific data processed, purposes, and retention periods are governed by the applicable Google and partner disclosures and may vary by configuration and your choices. We do not receive unrestricted access to the personal information processed by Google for advertising.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google's information about advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage consent and related privacy choices before requesting ads as permitted by the applicable SDK consent state. You may decline optional consent. Available ad formats and personalization may depend on your choices and applicable requirements.

If the Game displays a privacy-options control, you can use it to review or change the available choices. You may also manage certain advertising preferences through your Android or Google account settings.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process information as necessary to provide game features, remember local settings, and maintain the Game. Where personal data is processed for advertising, the relevant legal basis depends on the purpose, applicable law, and the choices made through the consent flow. Where consent is legally required, optional processing should not rely on consent that has not been obtained. Other processing may be necessary for security, fraud prevention, or compliance with legal obligations, as permitted by law.

5. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners in connection with the AdMob service. These providers may process data in countries outside your country of residence, subject to their applicable privacy terms and transfer safeguards.

6. Data retention

Locally stored gameplay data generally remains on your device until it is cleared, the Game is uninstalled, or a game feature removes it. Third-party advertising data is retained according to the relevant provider's policies and your applicable choices. We do not control Google's independent retention schedules.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal data, withdraw consent, or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. To contact us about data handled by YORU Studio, email com.yorustudio@gmail.com. For data controlled directly by Google, you may also need to contact Google or use its privacy controls.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not specifically designed for children. Advertising and age-related requirements will be applied according to the Game's final age rating, intended audience, and applicable platform policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us at the email below.

9. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's features, SDKs, advertising practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date.
